# SPRIGLET - BATTLE SPRITE

## REFERENCE

Use spriglet_master.png as the primary visual reference.

Preserve exactly:
- head shape
- eye shape
- leaf-shaped ears
- leafy head crest
- light green fur
- dark green accents
- cream chest
- branch-like tail
- three tail leaves
- body proportions

Do not redesign the creature.

## BATTLE POSE

Create a dynamic three-quarter battle stance.

Spriglet should look ready to attack.

Pose:
- body slightly lowered
- one front paw raised
- energetic stance
- confident expression
- tail visible
- leaves moving slightly with the motion

## ART STYLE

2D fantasy RPG battle sprite.
Polished game artwork.
Clean readable silhouette.
Smooth cel shading.
Full body.
Strong silhouette.

## BACKGROUND

Transparent background.

No:
- scenery
- text
- UI
- other monsters
- trainer

## OUTPUT

High-resolution PNG.
Full body.
Centered.
Suitable for a battle screen.
