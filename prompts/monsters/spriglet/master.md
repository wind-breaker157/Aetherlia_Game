# AETHERLIA - SPRIGLET
# MASTER CHARACTER DESIGN

## 1. CHARACTER IDENTITY

Name: Spriglet
Species ID: spriglet
Monster No: 001
Type: Nature
Role: Speed / Support
Habitat: Forest

Personality:
- Friendly
- Curious
- Energetic
- Playful
- Brave

## 2. BODY DESIGN

Spriglet is a small quadruped fantasy forest creature.

Body:
- Compact body
- Small and agile proportions
- Four short legs
- Rounded torso
- Slightly larger head compared to body
- Cute but capable of moving quickly

Fur:
- Light green main fur
- Darker green accents
- Cream-colored chest
- Cream-colored muzzle

## 3. HEAD DESIGN

Eyes:
- Large expressive emerald-green eyes
- Bright highlights
- Friendly expression

Ears:
- Large leaf-shaped ears
- Natural pointed leaf tips
- Dark green outer areas
- Lighter green inner areas

Head Crest:
- A layered leafy crest grows from the top of the head
- The crest is made from several leaves
- Leaves follow the natural shape of the head
- The crest is one of Spriglet's most recognizable features

## 4. TAIL DESIGN

Tail:
- Branch-like tail
- Natural wooden texture
- Slight upward curve
- Three fresh green leaves attached to the tail
- Tail must remain visible in three-quarter views

## 5. COLOR PALETTE

Main fur:
Light green

Secondary fur:
Medium green

Dark accents:
Dark forest green

Chest:
Warm cream

Eyes:
Emerald green

Tail:
Natural brown

Leaves:
Fresh green

Overall palette:
Fresh forest green, cream, natural brown and emerald.

## 6. NATURE IDENTITY

Spriglet is a Nature-type monster.

Nature visual elements:
- Leaves
- Small branches
- Vines
- Forest growth
- Natural energy

Do not make the Nature identity depend only on green color.

The creature must be recognizable by its physical design,
not simply by recoloring.

## 7. SILHOUETTE

The silhouette must be recognizable when displayed at small size.

Important silhouette features:
- Large leaf ears
- Leafy head crest
- Compact quadruped body
- Branch-like tail
- Three tail leaves

The silhouette should remain readable
without facial details.

## 8. PROPORTIONS

Head:
Large relative to body.

Body:
Small and compact.

Legs:
Short but agile.

Tail:
Long enough to remain visible behind the body.

Ears:
Large and visually important.

Overall impression:
Cute, agile, young forest creature.

## 9. FACE

Default expression:
Friendly and curious.

Mouth:
Small smiling mouth.

Facial proportions:
Large eyes,
small muzzle,
soft rounded facial structure.

Avoid:
Aggressive facial structure,
overly realistic anatomy,
human facial features.
